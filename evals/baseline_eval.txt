CLASSICAL ML BASELINE BAKE-OFF -- TF-IDF + logistic regression
==============================================================

Train : 300 DVIDS snippets, Opus-JUDGE labels (not human; the
        baseline inherits the judge's ~5-6% human-disagreement ceiling).
Test  : the 54-row human gold set, touched once. Region is NOT covered
        (no region labels exist in the training data until v3.1.0).
Config: word 1-2 grams, english stop words, min_df=2,
        LogisticRegression(class_weight='balanced') per axis.
Caveat: 'industry' has n=1 training row -- structurally unlearnable.
